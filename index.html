Welcome to A Moment of Syzygy

A Moment of Syzygy is dedicated to helping you learn basic topics and terms in astronomy. The glossary page will cover terms you might not find in the mini topic lessons. The practice page will walk you through how to stargaze from your computer and what to look for to deepen your understanding of astronomy. We hope we'll walk away with a fun fact or two!
